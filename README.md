### **Popular Video Formats**
1. **MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14)** – The most widely used format, offering excellent compression and compatibility across devices and platforms. Ideal for streaming, social media, and general video playback[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://www.downelink.com/12-different-types-of-video-files-and-formats/?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "1")[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
2. **AVI (Audio Video Interleave)** – An older format developed by Microsoft. It supports high-quality video but results in large file sizes. Best for archival purposes or editing[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://www.downelink.com/12-different-types-of-video-files-and-formats/?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "1").
3. **MKV (Matroska Multimedia Container)** – A flexible format that supports multiple audio and subtitle tracks. Great for storing high-quality movies and TV shows[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://www.downelink.com/12-different-types-of-video-files-and-formats/?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "1").
4. **MOV (Apple QuickTime Movie)** – Developed by Apple, this format provides high-quality video and is commonly used for professional editing and Apple devices[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
5. **WMV (Windows Media Video)** – Created by Microsoft, WMV offers good compression but is mainly used for Windows-based applications and streaming[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
6. **FLV (Flash Video Format)** – Previously popular for web video, especially on Adobe Flash-based platforms. Less common today due to Flash being phased out[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
7. **WebM** – Designed for web use, WebM is optimized for HTML5 video and offers efficient compression for online streaming[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
8. **MPEG (Moving Picture Experts Group)** – A family of formats used for DVDs and broadcasting. MPEG-2 is common for DVDs, while MPEG-4 is used for online video[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
9. **3GP** – A mobile-friendly format designed for older phones with limited storage and processing power[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
10. **AVCHD (Advanced Video Coding High Definition)** – Used for high-definition camcorder recordings, offering excellent quality with efficient compression[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://dexonsystems.com/blog/video-file-formats?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").
